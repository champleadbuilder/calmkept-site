What paperwork should you keep after buying a house?

Keep the closing documents, the mortgage details, the homeowner's insurance policy, property tax records, the inspection report, and the warranties and manuals that came with the house. Then add the details you learn as you live there: service providers, and the maintenance schedule.

From the closing

  • The full closing package, and a note of where the signed originals are kept
  • The deed, or the record of where it's held
  • The mortgage: lender, loan number, payment date, escrow details
  • Homeowner's insurance: insurer, policy number, renewal date
  • Property tax records, and any exemption you've applied for
  • HOA or condo documents, if there are any

About the house itself

  • The inspection report, and the known issues it listed
  • Systems inventory: the age and model of the furnace, water heater, roof and major appliances
  • Warranties and manuals: what's covered, and until when
  • Paint and materials: colors and finishes, for the next touch-up
  • Service providers: who you call for what

Turning it into a calendar

Put the recurring dates in one place: mortgage, insurance renewal, tax due dates, HOA dues, and seasonal maintenance.
