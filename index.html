Why Calmkept exists

Most preparedness products lean into fear. Calmkept doesn't. The unglamorous work of getting your affairs in order shouldn't feel like preparing for the worst — it should feel like what it actually is, a quiet act of love for the people who'd otherwise have to guess.

We make printable binders and cards that gather the things your family would need to know — medical information, accounts, where important documents live, final wishes, the household details only you carry in your head — into one organized place. Practical for real life. Beautiful enough to actually use. Free of the alarmist tone most preparedness products carry.

Calmkept is for new parents, adult children caring for aging parents, recent homeowners, and anyone who believes being prepared is its own kind of care. Take your time. You're doing a kind thing.

A calm place to start

The free Emergency Starter Checklist: five small steps, most under ten minutes. A conversation worth having, the documents your family should be able to find today, a one-page call list, a quick photo of your medications, and a five-minute weekly check-in.

Questions

What do I get?

A PDF you download right after you pay. Nothing is shipped. You print it and fill it in by hand, at your own pace.

How do I print it?

At home or at a copy shop. Each listing says which paper sizes are included: US Letter (8.5 × 11 in), A4, or both. When your details change, print a fresh page.

Can I get a refund?

Because the files download straight away, we don't offer change-of-mind refunds once the download has begun. If you're in the UK or EU, you agree to this before you pay: on Payhip by ticking a box at checkout, and on Etsy through the notice in each listing. The refund policy explains it in full. If a file won't download, is corrupted, or isn't as described, email care@calmkept.com and we'll replace it or refund it. Your rights for a faulty file are never affected.

Etsy or Payhip: which store should I use?

The one for where you live. If you're in the United States or Australia, shop on Etsy. Anywhere else, shop on Payhip. The Shop link at the top of this page takes you to the right one.
