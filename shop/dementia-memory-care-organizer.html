Dementia & Memory Care Organizer

Caring for someone living with dementia or another memory condition means holding a lot of small, specific facts — what calms them, what confuses them, what the morning routine looks like — that are easy to lose between one caregiver and the next, or between one hard week and the next. This organizer is built to hold that record in one place. The Dementia & Memory Care Organizer is a calm, print-and-write record for day-to-day memory care. It holds what you've noticed and what's in place. It does not tell you how to care for someone with dementia, it does not diagnose or characterize the condition, and it does not instruct on technique. A record you keep, not medical advice.

This organizer is not yet available to buy. It holds day-to-day care records only; legal and financial paperwork is outside its scope. Questions? care@calmkept.com
