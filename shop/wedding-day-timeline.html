Wedding Day Timeline

A wedding day runs on dozens of small handoffs — a photographer who needs fifteen minutes that aren't in anyone's head but yours, a flower delivery that has to beat the ceremony, whoever is holding the rings, not sure when to hand them over. This is one place to put the whole day in order, so the people running it can run it without asking you. The Wedding Day Timeline is a calm, fill-in schedule for the wedding day itself — not the months of planning before it. It holds the hour-by-hour order of the day, who's involved, and who to ask instead of the couple. This organizes the schedule. It doesn't plan the wedding or replace what you work out directly with your vendors and venue.

This timeline is not yet available to buy. Built for any couple, no assumptions about who's involved or how the day is structured. Questions? care@calmkept.com
