Home Maintenance Binder

A house asks for small, steady attention — a filter here, a gutter there, a service call every so often. Miss enough of it and the small things become the expensive ones. The Home Maintenance Binder is one calm place to keep what the house has, what has been done to it, and what is due next — the systems and their model numbers, the seasonal rounds, the service history, the warranties, and the people who keep it all running.
