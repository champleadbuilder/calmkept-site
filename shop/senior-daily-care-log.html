Senior Daily Care Log

Caring for an aging parent or relative day to day means a lot of small facts that are easy to lose track of between one caregiver and the next — what was eaten, what medication was given, how the morning went. This log is built to hold that record in one place, so it doesn't all have to be carried in memory or repeated from scratch to whoever is helping tomorrow. The Senior Daily Care Log is a calm, simple place to record each day of care — what happened, what was given, how they seemed. It holds what happened. It does not judge a day as good or bad, and it does not tell you what to do differently. A record you keep, not medical or care advice.

This log is not yet available to buy. The medication pages are a record only: follow the directions from the prescriber or pharmacist. Questions? care@calmkept.com
