Family Emergency Binder

For the sitter, the trip, or a parent in the hospital: contacts, medical info, and key documents in one binder your family can grab and follow. The Family Emergency Binder is a printable PDF that gathers the details your loved ones would need if you were ever unreachable, unwell, or gone — medical information, accounts, insurance, passwords, home and pet care, legal documents, and final wishes — into one clear binder they can actually find and follow. It is a quiet act of love: the answer to "where do I even start?" so the people you care about aren't left guessing during a hard moment.
